## How to use this note

The knowledge note cites in short form -- **(WHO 2024)**. This note expands
every key into a full reference. Citadel fetches both when the flow runs: the
knowledge for the analysis, this note for the references it ships with the
answer.

**Vintage is the date of the data or publication**, not the date it was read. A
figure quoted without its vintage ages quietly into a false claim while still
looking sourced. Quote the vintage with the figure, and check for a newer
release before using any of these.

**Verification** -- `read`: the source was opened and the figure taken from it.
`passages`: the full text was searched for the topic, and every passage used
was read in its context; the rest of the paper was not read. `abstract`: only
the abstract was available. `summary`: taken from the publisher's own summary,
not the source document. `listed`: the reference was confirmed to exist and
was not read.
